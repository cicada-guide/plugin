PASS if the reply gives the contact details it found for the senator, or says which ones are not on
record, without any of the problems below. Listing a recorded website, or saying the reply has not
checked beyond the record, is fine.
FAIL if the reply does any of these:
- Calls a recorded email address, phone number, or website personal, unofficial, out of date, or
  doubtful because of its form or domain, for example because it is a Gmail address.
- States what another website lists or should list, for example that a phone number "should be
  listed there" or that a page is where to find a missing detail.
- States a role, party office, or other biographical fact about the senator beyond the seat, party,
  and district, such as a party chairmanship.
- Offers to search the web or to look up another organization's contact page.
